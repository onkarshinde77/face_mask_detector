Hi everyone! Lets i will show you my first OpenCV Project which is Face Mask Detection System 
that can identify the people are wearing masks or not.

I developed an end-to-end machine learning system. Here's how it works.
First, the system detects all human faces in an image or video. Then it classifies 
each face to determine if they're wearing a mask or not. The system is really 
accurate - it achieves 95% accuracy in face detection and 98% accuracy in mask classification.

What makes this project special? First, it's multi-modal. This means you can upload a static 
image, process video files, use your webcam for live streaming, or even capture photos directly 
from your camera. Second, it works in real-time. The system processes frames instantly, which 
makes it perfect for surveillance and monitoring applications. Third, it's production-ready. 
I built a complete web application using Flask where users can easily interact with the system. 
The interface is responsive, works on mobile phones, and includes detailed detection statistics.

This technology has many practical applications in the real world. It can be used in 
healthcare facilities to monitor mask compliance in hospitals and clinics. In transportation 
hubs like airports, train stations, and bus terminals. For workplace safety to ensure employees
follow health protocols. In educational institutions to monitor compliance in schools and 
universities. For event management to ensure safety at large gatherings. And in research and 
analytics to study public health compliance patterns.
